Standardized Victim Reporting for Incidents and Basic Emergency Response

SCRIBER is a product of the UP Manila Disaster Risk Reduction and Management in Health Program, in collaboration with the Department of Science and Technology, and the Philippine Council for Health Research and Development. The mobile and web applications were developed to improve the disaster documentation process in the Philippines by adopting the Utstein template for uniform data reporting in acute medical response during disasters.

SCRIBER emphasizes the systematic documentation of victims and emergency response activities, highlighting the application's holistic role in promoting consistent, reliable, and standardized disaster reporting. Hence the application's name: Standardized Victim Reporting for Incidents and Basic Emergency Response. The development process of the tool started in 2026 by engaging with software developers from TechFactors Inc. The tools were developed primarily referencing the disaster medical response data elements elaborated in Debacker et al. (2012)1.
